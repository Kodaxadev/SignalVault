# Feature-to-Gameplay Map

Every Signal Vault feature must tie to an EVE Frontier action.

## Field Signals

### In-Game Trigger

Player observes something worth remembering.

### Examples

- Gate blocked
- Gate passed
- Permit required
- Toll suspected
- Hostile nearby
- Storage accessible
- Storage denied
- Market closed
- Route safe
- Route unsafe
- Resource found

### Gameplay Value

Turns field observations into searchable and linked intel.

## Object Dossiers

### In-Game Trigger

Player opens Signal Vault from a Smart Assembly or selects an object.

### Gameplay Value

Shows all known Signals attached to the object.

## Gate Dossier

### In-Game Trigger

Player interacts with or approaches a Smart Gate.

### Questions Answered

- Is the gate open?
- Is it permit-based?
- Is there a toll?
- Who owns it?
- Did someone recently fail?
- Is the exit hostile?
- Is the route stale?

## Storage Dossier

### In-Game Trigger

Player interacts with a Smart Storage Unit.

### Questions Answered

- What is this storage for?
- Is access public, private, tribe, or unknown?
- What was last known to be inside?
- Is the manifest manual or confirmed?
- Is the manifest stale?
- Did someone get denied access?

## Market Watch

### In-Game Trigger

Player visits/checks a market or trading location.

### Questions Answered

- Is the market open?
- Is it closed?
- Does it have useful liquidity?
- Is it hostile?
- Is this worth hauling to?

## Route Ledger

### In-Game Trigger

Player scouts, hauls, or plans movement.

### Questions Answered

- Is this route safe?
- Which hop is risky?
- Which gate reports are stale?
- Is the market/storage stop still useful?
- Has the route been verified recently?

## System Dossier

### In-Game Trigger

Player enters or plans around a system.

### Questions Answered

- Have I been here before?
- Are there warnings?
- Are there known gates?
- Is there known storage?
- Is there market intel?
- What tribe/player activity has been observed?

## Player / Tribe Dossiers

### In-Game Trigger

Player encounters another player, tribe, gate owner, market operator, or trader.

### Questions Answered

- Friendly, neutral, hostile, or unknown?
- Reliable trader?
- Known gate/storage owner?
- Prior incidents?
- Subjective trust notes?

## Confidence

### Gameplay Value

Prevents acting on fake certainty.

States:

- Unknown
- Rumor
- Observed
- Corroborated
- Verified
- Stale
- Contradicted

## Staleness

### Gameplay Value

Highlights that intel decays.

Examples:

- hostile contact stale after hours
- market report stale after a day
- route report stale after several days
- storage manifest stale after a week

## Contradiction Surfacing

### Gameplay Value

Preserves conflicting reports as signal.

Example:

- Signal A says gate passed
- Signal B says gate blocked
- Signal Vault marks gate as contested/re-scout needed

## Tribe Vault

### Gameplay Value

Turns individual notes into shared operational memory.

Scopes:

- private
- tribe
- officer
- scout cell
- public

## In-Game Access Code

### Gameplay Value

Lets players authenticate inside the in-game browser even if wallet extension/session is unavailable.
